Chicken Curry

Description

This flavorful chicken curry is rich, creamy and packed with aromatic spices. It's perfect served over steamed rice for a comforting dinner.

The recipe is simple enough for weeknight cooking but impressive enough for guests.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into chunks
  • 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons fresh ginger, grated
  • 2 tablespoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder (adjust to taste)
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish
  • Cooked rice for serving

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onions and cook until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add garlic and ginger and cook for another minute until fragrant.
  3. Add all the spices (curry powder, turmeric, cumin, coriander, and chili powder). Stir and cook for 30 seconds to toast the spices.
  4. Add the chicken pieces and cook until they start to brown, about 5 minutes.
  5. Add the diced tomatoes with their juice, coconut milk, and chicken broth. Stir to combine.
  6. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low. Cover and cook for about 20-25 minutes until the chicken is tender and cooked through.
  7. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  8. Serve hot over steamed rice, garnished with fresh cilantro.
