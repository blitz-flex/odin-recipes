Caesar Salad

Description

This classic Caesar salad recipe features crisp romaine lettuce, homemade croutons, Parmesan cheese, and a creamy, tangy dressing.

It's the perfect side dish for almost any meal, but can also stand alone as a light lunch or dinner when topped with grilled chicken or shrimp.

Ingredients

  • 2 heads romaine lettuce, chopped
  • 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 cup croutons
  • 1/3 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, Worcestershire sauce, mustard, salt, and pepper to create the dressing.
  2. Wash and dry the romaine lettuce thoroughly.
  3. Tear or chop the lettuce into bite-sized pieces and place in a large salad bowl.
  4. Pour the dressing over the lettuce and toss gently to coat.
  5. Sprinkle with croutons and grated Parmesan cheese.
  6. Toss once more before serving.
