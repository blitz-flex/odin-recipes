Strawberry Cheesecake

Description

This creamy strawberry cheesecake has a buttery graham cracker crust and is topped with fresh strawberry sauce. It's the perfect dessert for special occasions or whenever you want to indulge.

While it takes some time to prepare and chill, the result is well worth the effort!

Ingredients

  • For the crust:
  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/3 cup butter, melted
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • For the filling:
  • 4 packages (8 oz each) cream cheese, softened
  • 1 1/4 cups granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 4 large eggs
  • For the strawberry topping:
  • 2 cups fresh strawberries, hulled
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons water

Steps

  1. For the crust:
  2. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C).
  3. In a bowl, combine graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and sugar.
  4. Press the mixture into the bottom of a 9-inch springform pan.
  5. Bake for 10 minutes, then set aside to cool.
  6. For the filling:
  7. In a large bowl, beat cream cheese and sugar until smooth.
  8. Beat in sour cream and vanilla.
  9. Add eggs, one at a time, beating just until blended after each addition.
  10. Pour the filling over the crust.
  11. Bake for 55-60 minutes until the center is almost set but still slightly jiggly.
  12. Turn off the oven, crack the door open, and let the cheesecake cool in the oven for 1 hour.
  13. Remove from oven and cool completely on a wire rack.
  14. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.
  15. For the strawberry topping:
  16. In a saucepan, combine strawberries, sugar, and lemon juice. Cook over medium heat until strawberries release their juices.
  17. Add the cornstarch mixture and cook until thickened, about 2 minutes.
  18. Cool completely, then spread over the chilled cheesecake.
  19. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes before serving.
